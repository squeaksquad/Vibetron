Current designMidnight Glass

The shipping VT-369: a black glass fascia with gold legends, glowing meters and a machined knob. Try VU CAL, OPERATE and TAME; they behave like the plugin.

Knob: machined aluminium with knurled skirt and spun face. Drag up/right to turn, or click a numeral.
VU CAL: 0 VU reference (−24 to 0 dBFS), rise, fall and overshoot, and a VU or PEAK needle. The PEAK lamps light above the reference.
OPERATE / TAME: OPERATE engages the mode; OUTPUT TAME is a −0.1 dBTP true-peak limiter with no gain-reduction display.

Explored directionWalnut & Brass

1970s studio outboard: oiled walnut cheeks, a brushed faceplate, backlit paper meters and a chicken-head selector. The faceplate lacquer changes per mode.

Feel: warm, familiar, premium. Reads well at small sizes.
Mode cue: champagne → oxblood → black lacquer with gold legends. A jewel pilot lamp and louvres add hardware texture.

Explored directionBroadcast Console

A 1960s transmission-desk module: hammertone enamel, rack ears, illuminated push-buttons, and the mode notes on a taped index card.

Feel: engineered, characterful, a little playful.
Mode cue: machine grey → BBC green → graphite navy, with a different lamp colour for each.
Tradeoff: the busiest texture of the three, and the loudest in a crowded session.
Meters default to 0 VU = −18 dBFS, 300 ms to 99% and 1.5% overshoot. A steady tone reads at its peak level. All of this can be changed from VU CAL on the current design.
